For Admins

Setting up your school

From creating your account to approving your first attendance sheet — this guide walks you through the full admin setup.

1
Register your school
Sign up at the landing page and enter your school name, district, and contact details.
2
Add staff to the Directory
Upload a CSV of teacher and parent emails, or add them one by one. They'll receive a claim link.
3
Create class sections
Set up Grade 5A, Grade 6B, etc., and assign an educator to each section.
4
Approve submissions daily
When teachers submit, you'll see them in the review queue. One click approves and notifies parents.
For Teachers

Taking attendance fast

Your daily workflow takes under two minutes. Open the app, mark your class, submit. Done.

1
Claim your account
Visit the Claim page, enter your school email and school name, and set your password.
2
Open your class roster
On the Teacher portal, tap your class. All students appear pre-marked as Present.
3
Toggle any exceptions
Tap a student's name to cycle them through Present → Late → Absent. One tap each.
4
Submit to admin
Hit Submit. It goes straight to the admin queue. You're done for that session.
For Parents

Checking your child's record

The Parent portal shows you everything the moment it's approved. No app download required.

1
Claim your account
Visit the Claim page, enter your email and your child's school name to unlock access.
2
View today's status
The dashboard shows at a glance whether your child was Present, Late, or Absent today.
3
Scroll the full timeline
Every session ever recorded for your child is listed chronologically — searchable and exportable.
4
Switch between children
If you have more than one child at the school, toggle between their profiles at the top of the page.
For Everyone

Installing AttendanceFlow as an app

01
Open in your mobile browser
Visit the AttendanceFlow URL in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS). No app store needed.
02
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
On Android: tap the three-dot menu → Add to Home Screen. On iOS: tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen.
03
Open like any app
The icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to open AttendanceFlow fullscreen — no browser bar.
04
Works on 3G, even offline
The PWA caches your roster locally. You can mark attendance even without a signal — it syncs when you reconnect.

Common questions

FAQ

What happens if a teacher submits the wrong attendance?+
The admin can reject the submission from the review queue. The teacher will then be able to re-submit a corrected record. All versions are logged in the audit trail.
Does the app work without internet?+
Yes. The PWA caches your class roster locally. Teachers can mark attendance offline and the record will sync automatically once a connection is available.
Can a parent see another school's data?+
Never. Institutional sharding ensures that every user — including admins — only ever sees data belonging to their specific school. This is enforced at the database level, not just by UI filters.
How long do invite links last?+
Permanently. Unlike many systems that use expiring invite tokens, AttendanceFlow's claim links are valid indefinitely. Staff can claim their account whenever is convenient for them.
How do I add a student mid-term?+
Admins can add students to the directory and enroll them in a class at any time. Teachers can also add students directly to their roster from the Teacher portal during or before a session.
