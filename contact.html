How claiming works

Your school admin added your email to the system. Follow these three steps to activate your account.

STEP 01
Go to the Claim page
Visit attendanceflow.ug/claim in any browser on your phone or computer.
STEP 02
Enter your details
Type the email your admin registered and your school's name to find your profile.
STEP 03
Set your password
Choose a password and you're in. Your account is active immediately.
